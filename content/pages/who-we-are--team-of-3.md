## Meet Your Three-Person Support System

Every client on the Team of 3 model gets three specific roles, not three generalists doing the same tasks. Here's how the work actually breaks down, built on the [expertise](/who-we-are/expertise) each role brings to the table.

### CFO-Level Strategist
icon: ChartLine

This role handles cash flow forecasting, budgeting, entity structure decisions, and the strategy conversations that usually only happen at companies large enough to employ a full-time CFO. They review your numbers monthly and flag risks or opportunities before they turn into problems.

### Controller
icon: ClipboardCheck

The controller manages the structure behind your financials: month-end close, higher-level account reconciliations, and the accuracy checks that make sure your statements hold up to scrutiny from a lender, investor, or the IRS. They're the quality-control layer between daily entries and strategic decisions.

### Staff Accountant
icon: Calculator

This person handles transactions day to day: accounts payable, accounts receivable, payroll processing, and data entry into your accounting software. Clean, current books mean the controller and CFO-level advisor are always working from accurate numbers, not last month's guesswork.

## Why Three Is Better Than One

A single in-house accountant is a single point of failure. When they're on vacation, out sick, or leave for another job, your financial visibility goes with them. The Team of 3 model removes that risk by design.

- Faster response times: with three people already familiar with your account, questions get answered without waiting on one person's calendar.
- Built-in redundancy: if your controller is out for a week, the CFO-level advisor and staff accountant already know your business and keep things moving.
- Broader expertise: strategy, accuracy, and daily execution each get a dedicated set of eyes instead of one person's strengths and blind spots.
- Continuity of service: turnover at Buss CPA doesn't mean starting over. Two other team members already know your books, your industry, and your history.

## Building Real, Lasting Client Relationships

Three people working on your account consistently get to know your business better than one overworked hire ever could. Your CFO-level advisor understands where you're trying to take the company. Your controller understands how your revenue and expenses actually flow. That familiarity compounds over time. After a few quarters, your Team of 3 isn't just processing numbers, they're noticing patterns: a vendor's prices creeping up, a slow season arriving earlier than usual, an entity structure that no longer fits your revenue.
