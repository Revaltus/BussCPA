## LinkedIn

Most Hartford contractors don't miss a compliance date because they forgot it mattered. They miss it because the date lived in an agent's email, a sticky note in the truck, and a folder on someone's desktop.

December is the fix. The ground freezes, the phone gets quieter, and you finally have time to look ahead. That matters because most deadlines carry lead time. An insurance renewal needs 60 to 90 days of review, and an auto-renewal clause can require 30 or 60 days of written notice. A license that lapses can stall a permit in March, right when you want to start moving dirt.

The post shows how to build one shared calendar, with an owner and an early reminder for every item: insurance and bond renewals, retainage and payment terms, licenses, South Dakota annual reports, and the January 15 and January 31 tax and payroll dates.
